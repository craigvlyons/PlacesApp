Places New — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Places New is a personal Android app for saving and organizing places. It has no account system, advertising, Firebase integration, or developer-operated backend.

Information stored by the app

Saved place details, notes, ratings, favorite-heart choices, colors, and organization fields are stored locally in the app's private database on your Android device. The app does not send this saved collection or your notes to the developer.

Location

Location permission is optional. If granted, the app uses an approximate or precise foreground location to bias place searches and show nearby map content. Search remains available without location permission. The app does not request background location or keep a location history.

Google Maps Platform

When you search for a place, select a Google Maps result, or view a Google map, the app sends the information required for that request to Google Maps Platform. Google's handling of that information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Search terms and map or location requests may therefore be processed by Google even though the app has no developer-operated server.

Backups

The app can export or import a portable backup only through Android's system document picker. You choose the destination or source, which may include a third-party provider such as Google Drive. That provider's privacy terms apply to files you place there. The developer does not receive those files.

Diagnostics

The app writes local Android diagnostic messages for stable operation and failure categories. These messages are designed not to include API keys, search text, place IDs, addresses, coordinates, notes, backup contents, or other saved-record data. The app does not transmit analytics or crash reports to the developer.

Data control

You can edit or delete saved places in the app and create your own portable backup. Uninstalling the app normally removes its private local data, subject to Android device-backup behavior. Keep a verified portable backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The updated date above identifies the current version.
